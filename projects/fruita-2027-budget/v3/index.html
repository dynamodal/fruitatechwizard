How the figures were checked

Figures were read from the draft's text by script and an automated check confirmed each appears on its cited page. Totals were reconciled: programs to departments, departments to funds, funds to all-funds totals, and each fund's balance roll-forward. The only gap in the source tables is a $2 rounding difference in 2025 totals.

"Money in" and "Money out" leave out $2.4 million that city funds pay each other, so nothing is counted twice; those groupings add up to the draft's totals within $225. Staffing counts (pp. 323–324) are images in the PDF and were transcribed by hand. Page numbers are the printed page numbers; in the PDF file, add 4.
